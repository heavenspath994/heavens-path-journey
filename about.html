Our Story

Passionate About Sikkim

Located in the picturesque village of Phadamchen in East Sikkim, Heaven's Path Journey was born out of a deep love for the Himalayas. We are a team of passionate locals who know every trail, every hidden waterfall, and every sunset viewpoint in the region.

Our mission is simple: to share the unparalleled beauty of Sikkim with the world, offering travel experiences that are not just trips, but lifelong memories.

Whether it's the historic Silk Route, the rugged terrains of North Sikkim, or the vibrant culture of Gangtok, we bring you closer to the heart of the mountains.
